# Iddhi Privacy Policy

Mists publishes Iddhi. This policy applies to Iddhi’s invitation-only TestFlight beta for iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Your diary stays on your devices

Iddhi works without an account. It does not send your diary to the developer, use advertising or tracking, or include an analytics service.

Your diary can include meals and nutrition, workouts, notes, weight, profile details, goals, and progress photos. Iddhi uses that information to show your log and progress. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Photo access is optional: you can take a progress photo or choose one through the system photo picker.

If you use the paired Apple Watch app, Iddhi exchanges logging requests, food shortcuts, and a diary summary with your iPhone. The Watch can hold pending entries until they reach the iPhone. Iddhi does not send raw Apple Health records or progress photos to the Watch.

## Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, Iddhi can read workouts, active energy, weight, height, and blood glucose. Iddhi uses those records to show your history and, if you enable activity credit, to adjust your calorie budget. Iddhi does not write to Apple Health.

You can stop future imports in Plan → Apple Health or revoke access in Apple Health. Stopping imports keeps copies that Iddhi already saved. Use Delete Imported Health Data to remove those copies from Iddhi. Your original Apple Health records and manual diary entries stay intact.

## Backups and exports

Iddhi marks the diary, imported Apple Health store, Watch queue, and full export folder for exclusion from automatic backups. This setting does not remove copies from older backups.

The regular diary ZIP leaves out Apple Health imports and their settings and lets you choose a destination in Files. A full ZIP that includes Apple Health imports saves directly to the Iddhi Exports folder under On My iPhone or On My iPad. Iddhi does not offer a cloud destination for that full ZIP. Any files you move or share from Files are under your control.

The separate diagnostics export contains the app version and build, operating system version, whether the app is running on a simulator, and Watch connection status. It does not include diary entries, photos, or Apple Health records. Iddhi does not send exports to the developer.

## Retention and deletion

Your diary stays in Iddhi until you delete it. Use Plan → Your Data → Delete All Local Data to remove your diary, progress photos, imported Apple Health copies, settings, and reminders from Iddhi. This does not remove ZIPs saved in Files, original photos, or original Apple Health records.

## TestFlight and feedback

Apple runs TestFlight separately from Iddhi. Apple automatically collects beta usage and crash information and shares it with the developer. This may include your name, email address, and device details. If you send comments, screenshots, or other feedback through TestFlight, Apple shares that material with the developer too. See [Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

We use invitation details to administer the beta and use beta feedback and diagnostics to answer questions and improve the app. We do not sell that information or share it with third parties. We keep the copies we control only as long as needed to administer the beta, respond to you, or investigate reported issues. Apple manages its own copies under its privacy terms.

## Contact

For privacy questions or to ask us to delete feedback or contact information we hold, email [info@iddhi.org](mailto:info@iddhi.org). You do not need to send your diary, progress photos, or Apple Health records with your request.
